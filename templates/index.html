About CareFinder

CareFinder – Smart Nearby Hospital Finder is a diploma project that helps people compare nearby hospitals quickly during an emergency.

STEP 1

Share your location

Allow location access or type your area.

STEP 2

Choose accident type

Road crash, burn, fall, sports injury, cardiac or breathing emergency.

STEP 3

Compare & go

See the best match, call the hospital or open directions.

⚠️{{ disclaimer }} Hospital locations come from OpenStreetMap; ratings, bed counts and availability are demo values or not available, so always call to confirm.
